My name is Andrey Lepekhin (*leh-PYO-hin*). 
I'm trying to figure out how to live a good, contented life. I experiment with different approaches, from applied psychology and mindfulness to more esoteric practices. This is my blog, where I spill my thoughts, share what I've learned, and talk about anything that interests me.  

Here's a little more about me.

### Meaningful conversations

I enjoy conversations that bring people closer. I've facilitated many of them over the years, one-on-one and in groups, including [Circling](https://circlinginstitute.com/), [Authentic Relating](https://www.authrev.org/what-is-authentic-relating) games, and other structured ways to talk honestly. If you like connecting below the surface, let's chat.

</section>

I work with software, systems, and the people around them. Over the last 15 years I've done a bit of everything, from startups to big companies like Yandex and DoorDash: business analysis, quality assurance, project management, software development, and leading teams of developers. Connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/{{site.footer-links.linkedin}}).

### Inner work

As a mentor, I help people get to the root cause of their challenges, rather than just battling symptoms like procrastination. We experiment together to find effective ways to reach their goals, without resorting to self-blame as motivation.
